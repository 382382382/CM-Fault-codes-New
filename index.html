CM FaultCode Lookup

PPE and HSE fault tables, VM DTC list. Data from Fault_codes_12_11_26.xlsx.

Hardware accepts PPE or HSE (the table) or a unit such as DDS, GEARBOX or WHEEL.

Partial codes work: P0087 or 157 returns every variant.

Fill in either box or both. Clear a box to ignore it.

Search log

This app keeps a log of the fault codes you look up. Each entry has the date, time and what you searched for, and it is sent to the company fault log spreadsheet.

If you allow location, each entry also records where your phone was when you searched. Your phone will ask for permission. You can change this at any time on the Log tab.
